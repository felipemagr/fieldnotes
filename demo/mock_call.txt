# Mock onboarding call, about two minutes: a Spanish consumer lender joining a new facility.
# ME is the engineer, CLIENT is the lender's head of data.

ME: Thanks for joining. How does your loan data reach lenders today?
CLIENT: We are a consumer lender in Spain with about forty thousand loans. Every month our finance team emails an Excel export from our loan system. We cannot change the format, the vendor owns it.
ME: What does the file look like?
CLIENT: One row per loan. Dates are day month year, and amounts use commas as the decimal separator.
ME: And repayments?
CLIENT: They come through Stripe. Stripe sends us webhooks when a payment succeeds or fails.
CLIENT: Two things to flag. Some loans have no days past due filled in, and we sometimes restructure loans but keep the same contract number.
CLIENT: Also, if our data is clean and on time, can we get a higher advance rate?
ME: Good questions. I will follow up on all of them.
